## B84 — ✅ CLOSED. `export VAR=x; make <target>` was silently defeated, and the FIX inverted `.env` vs `.env.state`

MEASURED 2026-08-09, closed 2026-08-16 (#657). GNU make lets a **makefile assignment beat the
